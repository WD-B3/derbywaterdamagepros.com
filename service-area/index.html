Water Damage Service Area Around Derby, KS

One emergency line covers Derby and four nearby places, with honest timing for each drive.

Where crews go

How coverage works

Dispatch decides which crew goes where, based on the address, the type of loss and who is free. Derby is the home base, so calls inside the city are the shortest drives. Calls to the towns below take longer, and dispatch will give you an honest estimate of the drive and the queue.

Derby neighborhood guides

Five local guides describe parts of Derby using landmarks and city information: Downtown Derby, High Park and Madison Avenue, Amber Ridge, Duck Creek and Crane Park. None of them claims official boundaries, because we did not find a source that sets them.

Derby, ZIP 67037

Derby had 25,625 residents in the 2020 Census and has its own permits desk for building, plumbing, electrical, mechanical and flood plain permits. It buys its water wholesale from the City of Wichita. Most Derby calls involve supply lines, water heaters, appliances and storm leaks. See the home page and the full service list.

Mulvane, ZIP 67110

Mulvane lies south of Derby across the Sedgwick and Sumner county line. Its railroad history left a mix of older and newer housing. Read the Mulvane page for the local details, including who to ask about permits.

Haysville, ZIP 67060

Haysville sits west of Derby and has a floodway and a creek that shape its flood rules. The city applies a 50 percent substantial improvement rule to repairs in a floodplain. Read the Haysville page before you plan a rebuild.

Wichita, ZIPs 67203, 67204, 67205, 67206, 67202 and 67201

Wichita is large, so dispatch asks for your ZIP code first. Metropolitan Area Building and Construction Department rules apply to permits there. Read the Wichita page for the ZIP list and the multi-unit notes.

Clearwater, ZIP 67026

Clearwater is the farthest town on the list, about 20 miles west of Derby by road, and it sits near the Ninnescah River. Read the Clearwater page for what to do while a crew drives out.

Drive times and what they mean

Drive time depends on traffic, weather and where the nearest crew is when you call. Derby calls are usually the shortest, and Mulvane and Haysville are close neighbors. Wichita varies a lot by ZIP, since the city is large and rush hour changes the route. Clearwater is the longest drive on the list.

Because of that, dispatch gives each caller honest timing instead of a fixed promise. Same-day response is available when crews can reach you. If a crew cannot arrive quickly, dispatch will tell you what to do in the meantime, so the water does less damage while you wait.

How the ZIP list was chosen

The list covers the home city, three nearby towns and the Wichita ZIP codes closest to Derby. The Wichita ZIP codes with the most residents are 67203, 67204, 67205 and 67206. ZIP 67202 covers the downtown core and 67201 is a post office box ZIP. We named them so callers can see whether their area is covered before they dial.

If a nearby place is not listed, such as a rural address between two towns, call anyway. Coverage depends on where crews are working that day.

Permits and offices by town

Each place has a different office for permits. The City of Derby handles its own. The Metropolitan Area Building and Construction Department, called MABCD, covers Sedgwick County and the City of Wichita, and it is contracted for Clearwater and Mulvane. Haysville has its own Planning Department. Ask the office for your address before rebuild work begins.

Seasonal patterns across the area

Winter freezes put pressure on exposed pipes in garages, crawl spaces and exterior walls across every town on the list. Spring and summer storms push rain through roofs and windows. Summer heat strains air conditioners, and condensate lines overflow. Because these patterns repeat, a short walk through your home before each season can prevent a late night call.

Dispatch is open all year, and holiday calls are answered the same way as any other.

Calling from outside the area

People sometimes call about a parent's house or a rental they own from another city. Dispatch can handle that. Give the property address, a phone number for someone who can open the door, and the source of the water if you know it.

What happens outside the list

If your address is not on the list, call and ask. A crew may still be able to reach you, and dispatch will say so plainly if it cannot. Do not wait to find out. Close the valve, document the damage and call.

Before you call

Have the address, the cross street, the source of the water and whether the power is safe. If you can, say how many rooms are wet. That information lets dispatch decide which equipment to bring. See the contact page for our address and the services page for what we handle.

Not sure if we cover your address? Call and ask.
