How to Choose a Water Damage Restoration Company in Derby, KS

You may have to choose in a hurry. This checklist helps you ask the right questions on the phone and spot the warning signs.

By the Derby Water Damage Pros team · Published November 9, 2026

Water damage rarely gives you time to shop, but a few questions on the phone can separate a prepared company from a risky one. This guide gives a short checklist you can use in the first call, followed by deeper questions for after the emergency. It does not rank any company. It helps you decide what matters for your situation.

Ask about licensing and insurance

Ask whether the company is licensed and insured, and ask for proof you can see. A company should be willing to share a certificate of insurance. Rules vary by trade, so ask which licenses apply to the work being proposed, such as plumbing or electrical.

Never accept a vague answer. If someone cannot tell you plainly, move on.

Ask about response and honesty on timing

Ask how soon a crew can come and what the process is after hours. Be wary of anyone who guarantees an exact arrival time, since traffic and other emergencies change schedules. Same day response when crews are available is a reasonable promise. A company that is honest about timing is more likely to be honest about everything else.

Ask about standards

Ask whether the company follows ANSI/IICRC S500, the industry reference for water damage restoration. A good company can explain water categories, drying goals and how readings are taken. See structural drying and dehumidification for what to expect.

Ask about records

You want photos, moisture readings and drying logs. Ask whether you will get them, and when. These records support an insurance conversation. Coverage decisions are still your insurer's.

Ask about scope and price

Ask for scope and price in writing before work starts. A trustworthy company explains what is included, what is not and what could change the price. A clear estimate lists tasks, equipment and exclusions.

Red flags

Be cautious about door knockers after a storm, pressure to sign immediately, requests for full payment up front and promises that insurance will pay. Be cautious about companies that tell you to avoid calling your insurer. Be cautious about reviews or badges you cannot verify.

Local presence

A company with a local address and a local phone number is easier to find later if something goes wrong. Check the address. Derby Water Damage Pros is at 317 N Rock Rd, Derby, KS 67037.

How to compare two calls

Write down what each company says about timing, scope, price and records. Notice who asks careful questions about the source of the water, the depth and the electrical risk. A company that asks is thinking about your job, not just booking it.

If two companies sound similar, ask each how it handles a change in scope. The answer reveals how they treat customers when the job is harder than first expected.

After you choose

Ask for a written agreement before work starts. Confirm the contact person and the plan for the first day. Keep a copy of the scope and the proof of insurance. Check in daily during drying, and ask to see the readings.

If something seems wrong, say so early. A good company will explain or adjust. A poor one will avoid the question, and that is information too.

What a first visit should look like

A crew should arrive with moisture meters, extraction gear and drying equipment, not just a clipboard. Expect questions about what happened, a walk through the wet areas and readings taken at several points. Expect a plain explanation of the plan and the scope and price before equipment is set.

Someone should explain what will be removed and why. Ask to see the readings that justify any demolition, and ask for photos before and after.

Insurance questions to put to a company

Ask whether the company will talk with your adjuster, and what records it provides for the claim. Be cautious about a company that says it will handle the entire claim or that promises a result. Coverage decisions belong to your insurer, and you remain the person who signed the policy.

Choosing locally in Derby

After hail or wind storms in the Wichita area, out of town contractors sometimes appear going door to door. Before you sign, check that the company has a local address and ask for references you can call. The Kansas Insurance Department Consumer Assistance line, at 800-432-2484, can answer questions about claims, and the City of Derby main line, 316-788-1519, can say which permits a repair might need.

Questions for after the emergency

Ask who will be on site, how often readings are taken and how you will be told if the plan changes. Ask who handles rebuild and permits. Ask how to reach the company after hours.

Matching the company to the job

A small leak may need one visit. A flood or sewage loss needs more equipment and care. See flood damage restoration and sewage backup cleanup for the extra steps.

Common questions

What should I ask a water damage company on the first call?

Ask about licensing and insurance, how soon a crew can come, whether it follows ANSI/IICRC S500 and whether scope and price are explained before work.

Should a company guarantee its arrival time?

Be cautious if it does. Weather, traffic and other emergencies change schedules, and honest timing is a better sign.

Can a restoration company tell me what insurance will pay?

No. Only your insurer can confirm coverage. A company can document the loss.

How do I avoid storm chasers?

Check for a local address, ask for references and do not sign on the spot.
