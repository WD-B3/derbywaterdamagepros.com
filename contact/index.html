Contact Derby Water Damage Pros

The fastest way to reach us is the phone. Dispatch answers 24 hours a day, every day of the year.

Call the emergency line

Phone: (316) 535-3098. We handle water emergencies by phone only, because a form cannot tell you to shut off a valve. Tell dispatch where the water came from, how deep it is and which town you are in.

Where we are

Address: 317 N Rock Rd, Derby, KS 67037. Email: contact@derbywaterdamagepros.com. Email is for paperwork and records, not emergencies, so call if water is moving.

Hours

The dispatch line is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including holidays. Crews are sent when one is free, and dispatch will tell you honestly how soon that can be.

What to have ready

Have your address, the source of the water if you know it, and your insurer's name if you plan to file a claim. Take photos of the damage before anything is moved. For help with the first minutes, see the water extraction page. For towns we serve, see the service area page.
