Water Damage Restoration in High Park and Madison Avenue, Derby, KS

The City of Derby names High Park and Madison Avenue among places with high water crossings. Here is what that does and does not tell a homeowner.

What the city says about this area

The City of Derby's floodplain page states that there are high water crossings and the potential for flooding around High Park, Madison Avenue and Kay Street near Riley Park. That is the only neighborhood-level flood statement we found from the city, and it is about road crossings and potential, not a count of flooded homes.

The same page says Derby has had three significant floods in the past 25 years, in 1993, 1995 and 2016, and that each affected different parts of the city. It does not say which streets, so this page does not claim that any specific home here was flooded.

Landmarks that place you

Smith Farm and St. Mary School, a designated landmark, is at Madison Avenue Central Park, 512 E Madison Ave. The Chip Site park sits at the northwest corner of High Park, at 2801 E James. Those two addresses give a reliable sense of the area. We did not find official boundaries for High Park or Madison Avenue as neighborhoods, so treat this page as an approximate description.

Heavy rain and water coming in

When rain is heavy and water reaches a house from outside, the water is treated differently from a burst pipe. It may carry soil and contaminants, and it may enter at a door, a window well, a foundation crack or a low room. Do not wade through it if it could touch outlets or appliances.

Standing water is removed first, wet materials are assessed, and drying follows. See storm water damage cleanup and flood damage restoration for the steps, and basement water cleanup if the water reached a lower level.

What to do while you wait

Stay safe

Keep away from water near outlets or the panel. Call the utility or 911 for electrical danger.

Do not use fans or a vacuum

Household fans and vacuums are not enough for contaminated water.

Document

Photograph the water line on walls and furniture when it is safe to be in the room.

Flood insurance in plain terms

The City of Derby says homeowners policies do not cover flood damage, that it takes part in the National Flood Insurance Program, and that new flood policies have a 30 day waiting period. It also reports a Class 7 Community Rating System rating, which gives residents a 15 percent flood insurance discount. These are city statements, so confirm details with your agent.

Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status not verified. Check your address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center. The city's floodplain administrator can be reached at 788-0301.

After the water is out

Drying continues until moisture readings are normal. Ask for the readings, photos and scope in writing, because they may help when you talk to your insurer. Rebuild work may need a City of Derby permit, so ask the permits desk at 788-6632, option 2, before you start.

Common questions

Is my house near High Park in a flood zone?

We cannot say from here. Check your exact address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center or ask the City of Derby, since the city names this area for high water crossings but gives no home-level data.

Does the city say these streets flooded?

The city names High Park, Madison Avenue and Kay Street near Riley Park for high water crossings and flood potential. It reports three significant floods citywide, in 1993, 1995 and 2016, without naming streets.

Is storm water safe to clean up myself?

Not if it could be contaminated or touch electrical equipment. Stay out and call for help, and do not rely on household fans or a vacuum.

Does homeowners insurance cover rain coming in?

It depends on how the water got in and on your policy. Rising water is usually excluded, so ask your insurer.
