About Derby Water Damage Pros

A local dispatch line for water emergencies in Derby and the towns around it, built around fast answers and clear paperwork.

What we do

Derby Water Damage Pros is a water damage restoration service based at 317 N Rock Rd in Derby, Kansas. We answer calls around the clock, send crews to extract standing water, dry the structure and keep records that help you talk to your insurer. We do not do fire or smoke work, and we do not offer mold removal.

How we work

Every job starts with a call. Dispatch asks where the water came from, how deep it is and whether the power is safe. Before work begins, scope and price are explained so there are no surprises. Crews are licensed and insured, and drying follows the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for professional water damage restoration, which sets out how water categories and drying goals are defined.

Where we work

Our home base is Derby, a city of 25,625 people in the 2020 Census. We also serve Mulvane, Haysville, Wichita and Clearwater. See the service area page for details and the services page for what we handle.

What happens when you call

Dispatch answers by phone, any hour. The first questions are simple. Where is the water coming from, how deep is it, and is anyone near electrical equipment? Those answers decide whether the first instruction is to close a valve, leave the room or call the utility.

Next, dispatch asks for the address and the nearest cross street, and tells you honestly how soon a crew can be there. Same-day response is available when crews are free. No arrival time is promised, because other emergencies and road conditions change the schedule.

What a crew does on site

A technician walks the wet areas with a moisture meter and writes down what is wet and how wet it is. Scope and price are explained before work starts. Then the crew pumps out standing water, removes materials that cannot be dried, and sets air movers and dehumidifiers.

Each day the technician records new readings. When the readings match the dry reference points in the building, drying is finished. The photos, readings and notes stay in your file, so you have clear records to share with your insurer.

Standards we follow

Professional water damage work follows ANSI/IICRC S500, the industry reference for restoration. It sorts water into three categories, from clean supply water to grossly contaminated water, and it describes the drying goals for each. Following it means the plan for a clean pipe leak differs from the plan for a sewage backup, and the crew can explain why.

We hold ourselves to simple habits. Explain the scope. Keep records. Be honest about timing. Leave coverage decisions to the insurer.

What we do not do

We do not handle fire or smoke damage. We do not offer mold removal. We do not decide insurance claims, and we do not promise that a claim will be paid. If your loss needs another trade, such as a plumber for a pipe or a roofer for a storm opening, we will say so and keep the drying work moving in the meantime.

Records you can use

Every job produces a short file. It holds the first photos, the moisture readings from each visit, a list of what was removed, and a note on when drying ended. You can share the file with your insurer, your contractor or a future buyer. A clear record often shortens the back and forth that follows a loss.

We keep records because the work is easier to trust when it is written down. Moisture readings are numbers, not opinions, and a drying log shows that the structure reached its goal.

What to do before you call: safety first

Close the water source, stay out of water near outlets and take photos. Then call. The details of each step are on the service pages.

Types of water we plan for

A clean water loss comes from a supply line, a tank or a fixture, and it is the easiest to dry. A gray water loss comes from a washing machine, a dishwasher or a sump, and it carries some contamination. A black water loss comes from sewage or from floodwater, and it needs protective gear, removal of porous materials and careful cleaning. Knowing which one you have changes the plan, the equipment and the timeline.

What we promise and what we do not

We promise a clear scope and price before work starts, and honest timing when you call. We do not promise a specific arrival time, because weather, traffic and other emergencies change the schedule. We do not decide insurance coverage. Your insurer does, and we help by keeping photos, moisture readings and notes.

Why local knowledge matters

Derby sits just south of Wichita and buys its water wholesale from the City of Wichita. McConnell Air Force Base opened in 1952 and the city grew around it. We have no verified data on home ages, so we treat supply lines, water heaters and appliance hoses as common sources of loss in any house. The City of Derby also handles its own building, plumbing and electrical permits, which affects rebuild work after drying. Knowing these details helps us give practical advice, not generic instructions.
