Emergency Frozen Pipe Water Damage in Derby, KS

A frozen pipe often looks fine until it thaws, then the crack lets go. If water appears after a cold night, shut the main valve and call the 24/7 line.

A frozen pipe is a supply line where water has turned to ice, expanded and stressed the pipe wall. Frozen pipe water damage in Derby, KS usually shows up when the ice melts, because the split that formed while frozen only starts leaking once flow returns. The water that follows is clean supply water, but it still soaks drywall, insulation and flooring quickly.

If you see a leak after a freeze, close the main shutoff, drain the line by opening the lowest faucet, and call dispatch. Do not use an open flame or a torch to thaw anything. Leave electrical panels alone if water is anywhere near them.

Frozen pipe emergencies: first steps

The line is open 24/7 for freeze calls, including nights and weekends. Same-day response is available when crews can reach you, and the call taker will ask where the pipe runs.

Close the main

Turn off the main valve, then open the lowest faucet to drain pressure out of the broken section.

Warm the space safely

Raise the thermostat and open cabinet doors under sinks. Never use a torch, grill or open flame.

Photograph the damage

Capture frost, the cracked section if visible, and wet surfaces for your records.

How common are freezes in Derby?

This page does not give a local freeze count because it has not been checked against National Weather Service normals, and a made up number helps nobody. What holds true anywhere in Kansas is that a pipe in an exterior wall, an unheated garage, a crawl space or an attic is far more exposed than one in an interior wall. Check your own exposed lines before the first hard freeze.

If your pipes stay warm and the leak is from another cause, a different page applies, such as burst pipe water damage for a split under pressure with no freeze involved.

Signs of a frozen or split pipe

  • A faucet that runs as a trickle or not at all in cold weather.
  • Frost on a visible pipe in a garage, crawl space or basement.
  • A wet spot that appears after a thaw, often on a ceiling or exterior wall.
  • Sound of dripping inside a wall once the weather warms.

How the job is done

Extraction comes first, then crews open the wet wall or ceiling where the split sits. Moisture meters and thermal imaging map the damp area, and wet insulation is removed because it holds water against the framing. Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers then dry the cavity.

The plumber fixes the failed section, and the crew documents readings until the structure is dry. If the water ran into an attic or ceiling, the ceiling water damage repair page explains the repair side.

What affects the price

Scope and price are explained before work starts. The main drivers are:

  • How long water ran after the thaw.
  • Where the pipe sits and how many rooms the water reached.
  • Whether insulation and drywall need to come out.
  • Number of drying days and equipment pieces.
  • Repair and rebuild work after the structure is dry.

Many homeowners policies treat a sudden freeze burst as accidental damage, but wording varies and some policies expect you to have kept the home heated. Check your policy and ask your insurer. Want your real number? Call dispatch and describe the room.

Thawing safely and what the standard adds

Never thaw a frozen pipe with an open flame. Warm air from a hair dryer is safer, and the water should be shut off first. ANSI/IICRC S500 guides the drying work that follows a split, including how readings are logged until the framing is dry.

Common questions

Do you handle frozen pipe emergencies at night or on weekends in Derby?

Yes. Freeze damage often shows up late at night when the house warms or the weather turns, so the line is open 24/7. Same-day response is available when crews can reach you.

Should I thaw the pipe myself?

A hair dryer on low or a warm towel on an unbroken pipe is a safe attempt, but never use a flame. If a pipe has already split, thawing it will release the water, so shut the main valve first.

Is the water from a thawed pipe clean?

It is normally clean supply water, called Category 1. It can degrade if it sits on materials for a day or more, which is why drying should start quickly.

Could a pipe burst again later?

A pipe that froze once can be weakened even if it did not leak. Ask your plumber to check the section and consider insulating exposed runs.
